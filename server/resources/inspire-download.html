Download Index polygons spatial data (INSPIRE)

Data showing the indicative extent and position of registered freehold properties in England and Wales.

This dataset was published on 6 October 2024. You can access the data until we update the files. New files are published on the first Sunday of every month.

You can:

  • download polygons by local authority
